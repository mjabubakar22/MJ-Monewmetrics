## Summary

The Hershey Company presents a compelling investment opportunity centered on international expansion potential. Currently, North America accounts for over 80% of Hershey's $11.17 billion in revenue, with international sales averaging only $949 million annually. 

This heavy domestic concentration, while demonstrating market dominance, creates a significant growth ceiling given the maturity of the North American confectionery market. 

The investment thesis hinges on Hershey's ability to replicate its North American success globally, particularly in emerging markets where rising disposable income and growing middle-class populations are driving confectionery demand.
