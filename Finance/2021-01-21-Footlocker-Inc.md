## Summary

Foot Locker operates at the heart of the explosive sneaker culture phenomenon, serving as a critical retail bridge between major brands like Nike and a passionate consumer base of sneakerheads and hypebeasts. The company's 3,000+ global stores account for 83% of total sales, with Nike products representing 65% of revenue, highlighting the strategic importance of their partnership. 

Despite facing pandemic-related challenges that limited revenue growth to just 1% in 2020, Foot Locker has demonstrated operational excellence with superior inventory management, achieving the industry's highest turnover ratio of 4.4x and reducing inventory days from 110 to 81 over the past decade.

The investment thesis centers on Foot Locker being a fundamentally strong business trading at an attractive discount. The company has built a resilient retail model with exceptional inventory management and strong supplier relationships, particularly with Nike, which provides competitive advantages in securing coveted releases that drive customer traffic.
