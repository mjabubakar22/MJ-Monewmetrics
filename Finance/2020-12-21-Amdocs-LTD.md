## Summary

Amdocs Limited (DOX), a leading provider of software and services for communications and media companies, stands at the forefront of the 5G and Internet of Things (IoT) revolution. With over 30 years of industry experience serving 250+ organizations across 80 countries, the company has established itself as the go-to vendor for communication service providers looking to monetize new 5G-enabled services.

The convergence of 5G technology and IoT presents a massive opportunity, with IoT spending expected to reach $1.1 trillion within 3-4 years, driven by applications ranging from smart cities and industrial automation to consumer devices and healthcare solutions.

The investment thesis is built on Amdocs' unique positioning as the essential technology partner for telecom providers navigating the 5G transition. The company's proven track record of superior capital allocation and operational efficiency, combined with long-term customer contracts that provide revenue stability, creates a compelling value proposition
